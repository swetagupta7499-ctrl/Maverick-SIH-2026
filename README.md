## 📌 Project Overview

Maverick is an **AI-based early warning and disaster risk monitoring system** focused on landslides and related hazards in the **North Eastern Region (NER)**.

The system aims to monitor environmental conditions, identify potential risks, and provide timely alerts to support better disaster preparedness and response.

## 🎯 Problem

The North Eastern Region is vulnerable to multiple hazards, including:

* Heavy Rainfall
* Slope Weakening & Instability
* Landslides
* Flooding & Waterlogging
* Road Blockage
* Traffic Disruption
* Connectivity Loss

## 💡 Proposed Solution

Maverick combines **AI, satellite data, ground sensors, and communication technologies** to monitor risk conditions and deliver timely warnings.

### Key Components

* 🛰️ Satellite Data
* 🤖 AI Models
* 🌱 Soil Moisture Sensors
* 📡 LoRa Technology
* 📊 Monitoring Dashboard
* 📱 SMS / Mobile Alerts
* 🌐 WhatsApp / PWA App
* 📍 Location-wise Data

## ⚙️ Technical Approach

The system focuses on monitoring important disaster indicators:

**Extreme Rainfall → Increased Risk**

**Landslide:**
Cracks → Tilting → Falling Rocks

**Flood:**
Rising Water → Overflow → Waterlogging

Collected data can be analyzed through AI models to support risk assessment and early warning.

## 📊 Dashboard & Alerting

The proposed dashboard provides **location-wise information** for monitoring high-risk areas.

The alerting system focuses on timely communication through:

* SMS / Mobile Alerts
* WhatsApp / PWA
* Standardized Alerting
* Community Notifications

## 🌍 Feasibility & Viability

### Technical Feasibility

Integration of **satellite data, AI models, soil moisture sensors, and LoRa technology** provides the technical foundation for the proposed system.

### Economic Feasibility

The proposed high-risk district setup has an estimated **capital cost of around ₹25 lakh**, covering 50 sensors, dashboard setup, and one year of cloud hosting.

### Social & Community Viability

The system aims to improve community awareness, preparedness, and timely response while helping reduce potential casualties.

### Scalability

The solution can be expanded through:

* Geographic Expansion
* Cross-Hazard Readiness
* High Scalability
* Robustness
